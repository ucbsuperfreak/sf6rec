Privacy policy

SF6 Recorder · Last updated October 1, 2026

Summary

SF6 Recorder runs entirely on your own computer. It doesn't send any of your data to the developer or to anyone other than Google, and only to do the two things you set it up to do: upload your recordings to your YouTube channel and email you a summary.

Google data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, you grant two permissions:

  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload): used only to upload videos you recorded to your own YouTube channel, with a title and a description listing your matches.
  • Gmail send (gmail.send): used only to send the session summary email to the address you entered in the app's settings.

The app can't read your existing videos, emails, contacts, or any other Google data.

What's stored, and where

  • Your Google sign-in token, settings, the list of your sessions and matches, and small images of character portraits are stored only on your computer, in the app's data folder.
  • Recordings are saved by OBS on your computer and, by default, deleted after YouTube confirms the upload.
  • To read match results, the app analyzes your recordings on your computer using Windows' built-in text recognition. Nothing is sent elsewhere for this.

The developer never receives your data, and the app contains no analytics, ads, or tracking.

Sharing

Your data isn't sold, shared, or transferred to anyone. The only data that leaves your computer is the video and description uploaded to YouTube, and the summary email sent through Gmail, both under your own account.

Google API Services User Data Policy

SF6 Recorder's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • Revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstall the app and delete its data folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\sf6rec) to remove everything it stored.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/ucbsuperfreak/sf6rec.
