SF6 Recorder

A small personal Windows app for Street Fighter 6 players.

SF6 Recorder runs in the Windows system tray. When Street Fighter 6 starts, it tells OBS (on the same PC) to start recording. When the game closes, it reads the match results from the recording, uploads the video to the player's own YouTube channel, and emails the player a summary of their matches.

How it uses Google

Each player signs in with their own Google account. The app asks for two permissions:

  • Upload YouTube videos, to upload the player's recordings to their own channel.
  • Send email on your behalf, to send the session summary to the address the player chooses.

It can't read, change, or delete existing videos or emails. Everything runs on the player's own computer; nothing is sent to the developer. Details are in the privacy policy.
